The problem

Learning is everywhere. Direction isn't.

Students don't fail from a lack of resources — they drown in them, then stall right after finishing a course, with no idea what comes next.

01 · Discovery

Which course, playlist, or roadmap is actually worth my time?

02 · Direction

I finished the course. What am I supposed to build or do next?

03 · Readiness

Am I actually industry-ready, or do I just feel done?

04 · Gaps

Which specific skills are missing for the role I want?

05 · Proof

Is my resume and portfolio actually good, or just complete?

06 · Opportunity

Which internship, job, or client actually fits where I am right now?

The solution

One connected journey, not six disconnected tools.

Every stage feeds the next — your roadmap shapes your assessments, your assessments shape your readiness score, your readiness score shapes what's recommended to you.

01

Choose a goal

Pick a target role — Frontend Developer, Data Analyst, whatever you're aiming for.

05

Resume + portfolio + skills combined into one honest number.

06

Earn

Matched internships, freelance gigs, and jobs — sized to where you actually are.
